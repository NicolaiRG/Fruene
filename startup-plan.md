# Fruene — Pop Up Piknik Stavanger
### Startup Plan | Founded by two women, ages 25 & 27

---

## 1. Concept

Fruene is a full-service pop-up picnic company based in Stavanger. We deliver, set up, style, and tear down curated outdoor (and indoor) picnic experiences for any occasion — so clients arrive to a ready-made dream and leave without lifting a finger.

Every setup is tailored to the client's chosen color palette and theme. We handle logistics; they handle memories.

## 4. Operations

### Roles (two founders)
| Role | Primary owner |
|---|---|
| Bookings, client communication, social media | Founder A (27) |
| Inventory, logistics, setup/teardown, finances | Founder B (25) |

Both share physical setup duties at events.

### Storage
- Start: garage/home storage for inventory (low overhead)
- Month 6+: small rented storage unit (~500–800 kr/month) as inventory grows

### Delivery
- Start with personal vehicles (estate car / van)
- Month 9+: evaluate branded cargo van lease if volume justifies it

### Setup time
- Target: 60–90 minutes setup, 30 minutes teardown per event
- Max 2 events per day initially (morning + afternoon slots)

### Booking flow
1. Client DMs on Instagram or fills web contact form
2. Founders send package options + availability
3. Client pays 50% deposit to confirm (Vipps or bank transfer)
4. Remaining 50% paid 48h before event
5. Setup day-of; teardown agreed time after

### Cancellation policy
- Cancel 7+ days before: full refund of deposit
- Cancel 3–6 days before: 50% of deposit refunded
- Cancel <3 days: no refund

## 8. Legal & Admin

- **Business structure:** Enkeltpersonforetak (ENK) per founder initially, or a joint DA/AS
  - Recommended: Register one AS (aksjeselskap) together for liability protection and cleaner profit split
  - Cost: ~6 900 kr to register an AS via Brønnøysund
- **MVA (VAT):** Register when turnover exceeds 50 000 kr/year
- **Insurance:** Innboforsikring for inventory + ansvarsforsikring (liability insurance)
- **Contracts:** Simple client agreement covering damage liability for equipment
- **Accounting:** Use Fiken (cheapest compliant Norwegian option)

## 11. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Weather cancellations | Offer indoor option at client's location; flexible rescheduling |
